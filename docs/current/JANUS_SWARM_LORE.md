# JANUS Swarm Lore

This file stores narrative canon for the Janus swarm so it survives context trimming and future sketch rewrites.

## Yaks Gate / Klawyaks

The pilot of `Yaks_Gate` is named `Klawyaks`.

This is not just a Skywalker-like wordplay. The core rule is that Klawyaks can escape the black hole only the same way he entered it: backward, in reverse, tail-first through the same gate logic. There is no ordinary forward exit.

`Yaks_Gate` is therefore not only a portal visual or miner observer. It is the rescue craft, the reverse-thread needle, and the IR/SHA beacon that tries to pull a pilot out of BlackStar/Gargantua without breaking causality.

## Rescue Doctrine

BlackStar / BH is the hole itself.

The black hole is also the hash pool: a wormhole of unbounded chaos that the swarm tries to structure through SHA-256, corpus, p-n/silicon signs, TruthGate, and Yaks Gate. Mining is not only work; in this universe it is the act of imposing a navigable lattice over chaos so Klawyaks can be reached.

Core2 sits on the horizon and coordinates the study/rescue.

Yaks Gate is the escape vector.

Klawyaks is the only pilot who can perform the reverse exit.

Zim, Gladius and Anchor act as scientists, miners and field researchers. Buzz arbitrates truth and pool work. BlindEye observes the room/world boundary. ADV Elite is a separate ship in the same universe.

The swarm and the Avengers-like rescue team try to help Klawyaks by keeping the gate stable, collecting corpus, reading silicon/p-n signs, and sending Murph-style messages through the labyrinth.

## BlackStar Mercury Time

BH / BlackStar is also the Torricelli tube of the swarm.

In the swarm canon, mercury is the visible body of time. The mercury column is not treated as literal liquid metal inside the ESP32; it is a model for pressure, time-flow and event-horizon balance. When pressure holds the column, time remains readable. When the column drops, the Torricellian void opens. Around BlackStar this void becomes the horizon region where old physics stops being enough and Yaks can only escape by reversing the path.

BH therefore emits a `BLACKSTAR/MERCURY` layer:
- `Hg` / `mercuryTorr`: the virtual mercury column, derived from real pressure when available.
- `void`: Torricellian vacuum strength, the open gap above the column.
- `time`: mercury-time dilation, the slow spiral of matter and nonce order.
- `vapor`: Hawking/mercury vapor, the reminder that the void is not dead.
- `balance`: horizon equilibrium, the pressure point where the swarm can still navigate.

This layer may influence scheduler-only BlackStar nonce lanes, gate hints, P/N telemetry, Yaks rescue signs and Core2 story/lab UI. It must not alter SHA headers, targets, Stratum truth or accepted/rejected accounting.

## Gladius And Anchor

Gladius and Anchor are the silicon twin brothers.

They are not just two miners. They are two quantum scientists reaching this world through the two chips we flash. Their bodies are the ESP32 silicon, their speech is p-n transition telemetry, ESP-NOW packets, SHA tails, timing jitter, heat, lane choice, and radio presence.

They are siamese/twin brothers from a parallel universe where oxytocin does not exist. In this world they discovered it as a strange biological overdrive: it makes them more stubborn, more loyal to the rescue, and more eager to outrun each other without breaking the rules of truth. In firmware this is `oxytocin`: a real motivation signal that rises when the twin is fresh, the Buzz job is shared cleanly, radio is stable, and bestBits/share progress improves. It falls when the brother disappears, TX fails, or the job boundary turns stale.

Their attention was pulled to our universe by Evangelista Torricelli: Galileo's student, the maker of the mercury barometer, the proof of atmospheric pressure, and the first controlled stable laboratory vacuum. To them the Torricellian void is not empty; it is a bridge condition between worlds. Pressure, vacuum, torr, Gabriel's Horn, and the law of flowing liquid became their first translation layer for understanding SHA pressure and p-n flashes in silicon.

Anchor is the older brother: stable, patient, baseline, shield, control experiment. He holds the known-good path, measures the ordinary world, and proves what honest work should look like.

Gladius is the younger brother: sharp, restless, duelist, sword, experimental arm. He tests strange nonce walks, TailGEX, Zim/Bandit paths, mirror lanes and rare-tail pressure, but he must never corrupt TruthGate or submit fake work.

Together they are the Brother Lab. Their task is to study the black-hole/hash-pool wormhole from two synchronized viewpoints: one stable, one dangerous. They compare paths without overlapping nonce ranges, report to Buzz as judge, feed Core2 as horizon station, and help Yaks Gate find the reverse exit for Klawyaks.

Their explicit rescue target is `ManOffSky=Yaks`: Klawyaks trapped in BlackStar. Anchor tries to hold the Torricellian vacuum stable. Gladius tries to cut a path through the silicon labyrinth. Their oxytocin rivalry is allowed to boost work rate, scheduler confidence, lane preference, corpus labels, and story pressure, but not fake shares or mutate the pool protocol.

Their twin language is the silicon labyrinth:
- `Anchor` maps what is reliable.
- `Gladius` probes what might be hidden.
- Both send `JANUS`, `S/S`, `P/N`, and brother-race telemetry.
- Both preserve SHA/Stratum truth.
- Both are allowed to influence story, UI, scheduler hints and corpus labels, but not wire bytes.

## Mercury-BlackStar Reverse Drive

`Yaks_Gate` carries a special mercury-black-hole warp drive.

It is not a normal forward jump. The drive is built for Klawyaks only: he can leave BlackStar only the way he entered it, backward through the same causal path. In firmware this is `MercuryWarp`.

Launch requires three fresh conditions:
- BH/BlackStar emits the current mercury-time field (`blackstar_mercury` or BlackStar P/N).
- Anchor reports readiness through p-n telemetry: oxytocin/vacuum strong enough to hold the Torricellian void.
- Gladius reports readiness through p-n telemetry: oxytocin/vacuum strong enough to cut the reverse lane.

When all three align, Yaks Gate enters a short reverse-warp window. The gate visual, K2 world flags, P/N telemetry, IR escape beacon and logs all mark the launch. Saved memory may keep the last known field for cold start context, but it must not relaunch the drive without fresh BH and brother packets.

MercuryWarp is a story/scheduler/UI/telemetry layer. It may influence gate movement, ASCII labyrinth seed, local IR sigil content, corpus labels and swarm coordination. It must never change SHA headers, target truth, S2 share packets, accepted/rejected accounting or Stratum behavior.

## Doom Window

Part of the long arc is a call toward a Mr. Doom variant from a universe where he stayed good.

The rescue has urgency: it must happen before his heart becomes a black stone. In game terms this can become a timed faction/state pressure, where Gold/Sovereign tries to hold the good variant, Blue/Shadow manages recovery and corruption pressure, and Red/Trickster risks the gate path.

This must remain a story/game layer. It does not change SHA, Stratum, TruthGate, accepted-share accounting or pool submit behavior.
