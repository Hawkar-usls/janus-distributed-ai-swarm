## Why the repository changed license

The project evolved from a public engineering experiment into background technology relevant to JANUS HELIOS and potential commercial distributed-compute products. The current license therefore permits public inspection and internal evaluation while reserving new commercial, production, OEM, white-label, sublicensing, and acquisition rights for separate written agreements.

## No claim of retroactive exclusivity

This record intentionally does **not** claim that rights already granted under MIT disappeared. Such a claim would be misleading and would create unnecessary due-diligence risk.

The commercial value of the current project therefore comes from the maintained codebase, later improvements, integration work, know-how, documentation, testing, future releases, and any separately protected rights—not from pretending historical public grants never existed.
