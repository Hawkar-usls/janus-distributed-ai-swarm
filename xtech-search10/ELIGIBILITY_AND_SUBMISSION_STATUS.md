## Why clarification is required

The detailed xTech|Search 10 RFI, Section II, states that eligible applicants are **small, for-profit, independent U.S. businesses**. It further specifies qualifying ownership/control and a maximum of 500 employees.

At the same time, the official xTech|Search 10 webpage currently contains a summary field under `Who Can Submit` that says **small and large U.S.-based and allied foreign country businesses**.

These statements are not equivalent. Because the technology owner/developer is based in Ukraine, this package does not assume eligibility from the summary field and does not attempt to bypass the detailed RFI requirements.

Official references:

- https://xtech.army.mil/competition/xtechsearch10/
- https://xtech.army.mil/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/xTechSearch-10-Competition-RFI_Final.pdf

On 2026-09-11, an English-language reply was sent in the existing official xTech email thread asking:
